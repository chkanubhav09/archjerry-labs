The two of us

The studio is built on how we actually are. One of us ships systems, the other draws the world they live in.

Founder

Arch

Anubhav Chakraborty. ECE student at MIT ADT Pune who builds hardware and software. Made the VayuDrishti weather app, the SeArch browser and ScamDefender. Arch is the structure: code, systems, how things work.

Co-founder

Jerry

Arch's younger brother, an 8th-grade artist. Drawing is his thing, and he decides how everything looks and feels. Jerry is the finish: design, imagination, and the eye that finds what's off.

"Built by Arch, broken by Jerry."
